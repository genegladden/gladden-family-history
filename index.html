How the marks work

Every person on the line carries one mark. The mark is about the link, so a well-documented man can still stand on an unproven step.

Proven
A record shows it, or it is the family’s own knowledge of the nearer generations.
Probable
The evidence leans this way. A record does not yet prove the link.
Unproven
Kept in view on purpose. No record proves the link.
